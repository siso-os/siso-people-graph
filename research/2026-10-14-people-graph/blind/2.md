## Surprises
- Simile (Joon Sung Park, the Smallville author) raised $100M in Feb 2026 and $200M in Jul 2026 at about $2B to sell "agentic twins" for market research. This is the largest funded people-twin company found, and it is a commercialization of the 1,000-person paper.
- ElevenLabs' Iconic Marketplace (Nov 2025) is a rights-holder-approved licensing model for famous voices. It is the cleanest consent pattern found for talk-to-a-celebrity.
- OpenAI reportedly removed a Dean Phillips campaign bot for voice imitation without consent. This is a platform-policy precedent.
- Meta AI Studio gives every creator a DM-answering twin, and Meta reportedly plans celebrity bots (report only, not confirmed).
- Google was reportedly building celebrity and YouTuber bots on Gemini (report only, not confirmed).
