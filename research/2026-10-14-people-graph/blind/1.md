# Blind-1 notes: people databases, bios, notability, quotes, interviews, social archives

Rows: `rows/blind-1.jsonl` (63 rows, all `verified: null`). Budget used: 39 tool calls. Every URL comes from a search result or a homepage named in one; no page was opened in full, so every row is search-summary level.

## Categories found
- **Identity hubs:** Wikidata (Q5 about 6.4M direct, about 10.3M with subclasses), VIAF, ORCID, ROR, Getty ULAN, Open Library, OpenAlex.
- **Bio datasets and encyclopedias:** Pantheon 1.0 (11,341 bios), HBR (7M+), Laouenan cross-verified set (2M+), Gergaud HAL set (1.24M), Wiki people (1.9M), ODNB (60k+), Deutsche Biographie (about 50k full bios), NNDB (40k+), Prabook, Biography Reference Bank (licensed), WikiTree (25M to 36M profiles), Find a Grave (13.7M memorials added in 2023).
- **Notability and attention signals:** Pantheon popularity score, Wikimedia Pageviews top-1000 (Pew used it for person rankings 2015-2025), Forbes Real-Time Billionaires (3,400+), OpenSanctions PEP (2.03M entities, about 757k people).
- **Quotes:** Wikiquote (about 168k articles as of 2014, stale), Quote Garden, They Said So, Zyla. Only Wikiquote is clearly open.
- **Interviews and transcripts:** Television Academy "The Interviews" (about 900 to 950), Computer History Museum oral histories (1,000+), Miller Center presidential oral histories, Web of Stories, British Library Voices of Science (100+), TV News Archive (captions since 2009), Tapesearch (878k transcripts), Audioscrape, Particle.
- **Legislators and speech:** ParlSpeech (3.9M, then 6.3M in V2), Irish Dail speeches (about 4.5M), ParlScot (1.8M), EveryPolitician (100 parliaments, status unknown), Popit, legislatoR (67k politicians, 16 countries), Congress bioguide, UK Parliament APIs.
- **Social archives:** Pushshift Reddit (651M submissions, 5.6B comments for 2005-2019; current access unclear), Bluesky Pisa/CNR dataset (4M+ users, 235M posts), OSoMe Bluesky and Mastodon archives, Common Crawl (8 PiB+).
- **Creator and music registries:** MusicBrainz (about 2.87M artists per a May 2026 mirror), Discogs (5M artists in 2017), Last.fm 2010 Zenodo set, IMDb name.basics (daily dumps, non-commercial), TMDB person endpoints.
- **Commercial people data:** Crunchbase (API enterprise-only), Fundable (366k people per vendor), OpenSanctions (paid for business use).

## Surprises
- Pew Research built a person-category ranking from Wikimedia pageviews for 2015-2025. This is a ready-made notability proxy with a public method.
- OpenSanctions PEP is much larger than expected (2.03M entities). Its Wikidata-derived subset alone is 183k.
- Miller Center released Obama-administration transcripts for cleared interviews in March 2026.
- Find a Grave reports 13.7M memorials added in 2023 alone, so growth is fast.
- Pantheon's popularity score rests on Wikipedia language editions and page views. Critics say this measures reach, not contribution.

## Gaps
- **Not searched:** Social Blade and other influencer rankings, Genius (no count found), Letterboxd (no official people index), Hansard, C-SPAN, Australian Dictionary of Biography, Kaggle celebrity sets beyond the few found, Rate My Professors, Crossref author data.
- **Missing counts:** Prabook, NNDB status, EveryPolitician status, ISNI, Discogs dump sizes, OpenAlex author count (the 2013M figure in the 2022 paper looks like a typo), TMDB person total, IMDb name.basics row count.
- **Stale sources:** Wikiquote (2014), VIAF cluster counts (2011 and 2013), Pantheon (2016 release only), Pushshift (2020 paper).
- **Unverified primary pages:** Most rows rest on search snippets or third-party summaries. Pantheon, Wikidata, Find a Grave, ORCID and MusicBrainz live counts need a direct check.
- **Licensing:** IMDb, TMDB, Crunchbase, OpenSanctions, Find a Grave and Pushshift have terms that limit reuse. Read them before ingest.
